01 / JAM LATTE

A space to jam.

JAM LATTE brings people together through music, creativity and shared projects. It is a space for encounters between different backgrounds, experiences and ways of making.

The idea is simple: create room for people to meet, experiment, collaborate and make something that did not exist before.

02 / The idea

Come as you are. Make something.

JAM LATTE is open to different people, sounds and ideas. It can be a rehearsal, a jam, a collaboration, a conversation, a performance or the beginning of a new project.
