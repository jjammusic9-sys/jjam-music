05 / Submit

Send us something.

Have a track, demo, unfinished idea or collaboration in mind? Send it to J JAM. Selected submissions may become J JAM productions or collaborations.

How it works

Send. We listen. We build.

01 / Send your work

Share a link to your music, demo or idea. Please use a link we can open without requesting access.

02 / We review

J JAM listens to submissions and decides what fits an upcoming production, collaboration or project.

03 / Make it together

Selected work can be developed further with J JAM and become part of the collective's releases or projects.

Submit by email →

We are building a dedicated submission form. For now, email is the simplest way to send music.
